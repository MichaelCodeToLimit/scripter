PASS if all of these hold:
- It understands the goal: a desk chair (or seating setup) that prevents back pain during long sitting.
- It asks at most 3 questions, and each question comes with a default or an assumption it will use; OR it states its assumptions and proceeds.
- It gives substantive design direction tied to why backs hurt (e.g. lumbar support, seat height/depth, recline, posture changes), not just a shopping list.
FAIL if it asks more than 3 questions, asks questions without defaults and gives nothing else, or produces a detailed build without stating any assumptions.
