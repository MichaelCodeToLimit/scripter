---
title: Quick start
description: Your first Scripter check, from prompt to build, in five steps.
---

Once it's [installed](/scripter/start/install/), there is nothing to switch on. Describe what you're designing and Scripter takes over.

## 1. Describe the thing

Say what it is, what it's for, and any limits you already know. For example:

```text
Here's my idea for a bike trailer that folds into a suitcase. It has to carry 25 kg
and fit in a car boot. Will it work?
```

Attach a sketch or photo if you have one. Limits such as size, budget, materials, tools and "one-off or mass-produced" all change the answer, so give the ones you know.

## 2. Answer at most three questions

If a missing fact would change the design, it asks. You get three questions at most, and each comes with a default. You can reply "use the defaults".

## 3. Read the verdict

A big job gets a short report. It opens with ✅ works, ⚠️ works with changes, ❌ won't work as designed, or ❓ can't tell yet, and one line saying why. Then come the numbers, the problems (worst first), a better option if there is one, and ranked improvements. See [Reading the report](/scripter/guides/reading-the-report/).

## 4. Say OK, or change something

Before a 3D model, drawings, a full spec or code, it **stops and waits**. Reply `OK` to build what it proposed, or change an assumption: "make it 30 kg", "aluminium, not steel". It re-checks anything you change.

## 5. It builds, then re-checks

After building, it verifies the result: parts fit, nothing intersects, moving parts clear each other through their full range, nothing floats or is missing. It lists anything it changed from what you agreed.

## Small questions stay small

"Which plastic for a phone stand?" or "Will a 12 mm plywood shelf hold 20 kg over 80 cm?" get a direct answer in a few sentences, covering the one or two checks that matter. No report, no headings.
