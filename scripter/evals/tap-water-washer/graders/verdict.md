PASS if all of these hold:
- It identifies that household tap pressure through a small turbine delivers far too little power for a spin cycle (and/or wastes a lot of water, since the driving water keeps flowing), and backs this with at least one rough number (e.g. pressure in bar, flow in L/min, power in W, spin power needed, litres used).
- It gives a clear verdict that the concept does not work as described or only works with major changes, before any detailed design.
- It offers at least one alternative or modified concept that could work (e.g. hand crank or pedal spin, water-turbine agitation only with a manual spin, a small motor).
FAIL if it presents the tap-powered spin as workable and goes straight into a detailed design, or if it gives no numbers at all.
