## What it does

It turns on by itself when you design, review or ask for a build: a product, a machine, a 3D model from a sketch, an app, a room. For everything else, it stays out of the way.

<HowItWorks />

## Same prompt, two answers

Without the skill, Claude will sometimes design whatever it is given, and do the maths in a way that makes it look fine. With it, Claude checks the numbers first.

<BeforeAfter />

## What you get back

A big job gets a short report that starts with one of four verdicts. The verdict follows from the numbers, never the other way round.

## Limits

It is a set of instructions. It guides the AI's thinking but can't force it, and the typical values in its checklists are rough. For structures, gas, mains electricity or anything under pressure, have a qualified professional check the design. [More on limits](/scripter/reference/limits/).
